How the closet stays full

01

Teachers request

The inventory list is the first screen they see, with a Request button on every item. Anything not listed can be requested with a product link and a needed-by date.

02

You approve

Approve, deny or edit the quantity first. Stock is deducted on approval, never before, and requests that exceed what's on the shelf are flagged so you see them right away.

03

The list reorders for you

Low stock adds "Order more tissues" to your to-do list with the pack size and saved product link. Mark it received and the right count goes back on the shelf.
